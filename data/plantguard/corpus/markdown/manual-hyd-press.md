# 400-Tonne Hydraulic Press (HYD-PRESS) Equipment Manual and SOP

**VINDHYA PRECISION WORKS: EQUIPMENT OPERATIONS AND MAINTENANCE MANUAL**
**ASSET CLASS:** HYD-PRESS (400-Tonne Hydraulic Press)
**DOCUMENT ID:** VPW-SOP-HYD-0400-REV4

### 1.0 OPERATING PARAMETERS AND ALARM SETPOINTS
1.1. All operators must monitor the HMI dashboard for the following thresholds. Deviations exceeding the 'Trip' limit require an immediate Emergency Stop (E-Stop).
1.2. **Vibration:** Normal range 1.2–4.0 mm/s RMS. Warning threshold 5.6 mm/s RMS. Trip threshold 8.5 mm/s RMS.
1.3. **Surface Temperature:** Normal range 38–66°C. Warning threshold 78°C. Trip threshold 92°C.
1.4. **Hydraulic Pressure:** Nominal operating pressure 210.0 bar. Low-pressure alarm setpoint 185.0 bar.
1.5. **Electrical Current:** Normal operating current shall not exceed 115 Amps. Trip threshold 135 Amps.

### 2.0 PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE (PM) SCHEDULE
2.1. The base preventive maintenance interval for asset class HYD-PRESS is 750 running hours.
2.2. **Primary Responsibility:** Hydraulics Department.
2.3. **Support Responsibility:** Mechanical Department.
2.4. Maintenance tasks must be logged in the CMMS within 24 hours of completion, signed off by the lead technician.

### 3.0 FAULT CODES AND TROUBLESHOOTING
3.1. **Fault 01 (Pressure Decay):** Caused by failure of VPW-P-00002 (Deep seal). Replace seal and verify cylinder integrity.
3.2. **Fault 02 (Thermal Overload):** Caused by debris in the cooling circuit. Clear intake and verify operation of VPW-P-00037 (Economy belt).
3.3. **Fault 03 (Positioning Drift):** Caused by signal loss from VPW-P-00009 (Left sensor). Recalibrate sensor or replace if signal persists in error.
3.4. **Fault 04 (Vibration Spike):** Caused by degradation of VPW-P-00013 (Camera bearing). Replace bearing and check shaft alignment.
3.5. **Fault 05 (Contactor Sticking):** Caused by pitting on VPW-P-00042 (Notice contactor). Replace unit; do not attempt to file contacts.
3.6. **Fault 06 (Hydraulic Leakage):** Caused by breach of VPW-P-00005 (Inside seal) or VPW-P-00032 (Almost seal). Inspect all internal gaskets and replace affected parts.

### 4.0 CONSUMABLES AND SPARE PARTS
4.1. Only the following approved parts shall be utilized for HYD-PRESS maintenance:
*   VPW-P-00002: Deep seal
*   VPW-P-00005: Inside seal
*   VPW-P-00009: Left sensor
*   VPW-P-00013: Camera bearing
*   VPW-P-00024: Account bearing
*   VPW-P-00032: Almost seal
*   VPW-P-00037: Economy belt
*   VPW-P-00042: Notice contactor

### 5.0 TORQUE SPECIFICATIONS
5.1. Main Cylinder Flange Bolts: 450 Nm, applied in a star-pattern sequence.
5.2. Hydraulic Pump Mounts: 85 Nm.
5.3. Bearing Housing Bolts: 120 Nm.

### 6.0 SAFETY-CRITICAL ACTIONS AND ENERGY ISOLATION
6.1. All maintenance involving the opening of hydraulic circuits, replacement of electrical components, or work within the press bed area is deemed safety-critical.
6.2. **Hazardous Energy Sources:** All HYD-PRESS maintenance requires mandatory isolation of:
    a) Hydraulic pressure (bleed down to 0 bar).
    b) Electrical power (Lockout/Tagout at the main disconnect).
    c) Gravity (insertion of physical safety block/pins in the press bed).
6.3. Any maintenance task involving the above sources requires a signed Permit to Work (PTW) issued by the Shift Supervisor, Sanjay Varma or designate. No work shall commence without visual verification of the LOTO lock.
