# Lockout/Tagout and Energy Isolation Standard

**VINDHYA PRECISION WORKS**
**STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: CONTROL OF HAZARDOUS ENERGY (LOCKOUT/TAGOUT)**
**DOCUMENT ID:** VPW-SOP-EHS-042
**EFFECTIVE DATE:** 14 January 2025
**REVISION:** 4.1

---

### 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
1.1 This document defines the mandatory procedure for the isolation of energy sources for all machinery, equipment, and systems installed at Vindhya Precision Works (VPW) manufacturing facilities.
1.2 Compliance with this Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) is mandatory for all direct employees, contractors, and visiting personnel performing servicing, maintenance, inspection, or setup where unexpected energization, startup, or release of stored energy could cause injury.
1.3 No maintenance task involving energy isolation may be performed on the basis of a recommendation from an automated system (including the PlantGuard AI Maintenance Suite or SCADA-driven diagnostic engines) without a competent person's manual verification and physical sign-off on Form VPW-EHS-042A.

### 2. DEFINITIONS AND AUTHORISATION
2.1 **Authorised Person:** An employee who has completed VPW EHS Lockout/Tagout Level 2 certification, holds a current physical competency card issued by the VPW Training Academy, and is designated on the plant Authorised Personnel Register maintained by the EHS Department.
2.2 **Affected Employee:** An operator or worker whose job requires them to operate or work in an area where equipment is serviced under Lockout/Tagout (LOTO).
2.3 **Personal LOTO Padlock:** A red, individually keyed padlock engraved with the employee’s payroll number, retained exclusively in the custody of that employee.
2.4 **Group Lockout Box:** A red steel lockbox utilised when three or more Authorised Persons are working on a single asset.

### 3. SIX-STEP ISOLATION SEQUENCE
3.1 All equipment shall be isolated using the following six-step sequence in exact numerical order. Omitting or reversing steps is a critical safety violation resulting in immediate disciplinary action.

*   **Step 1: Notification**
    3.1.1 The Authorised Person shall notify all Affected Employees that equipment shutdown and isolation is scheduled, stating the asset ID, expected duration, and affected zone no later than fifteen (15) minutes prior to isolation.

*   **Step 2: Shutdown**
    3.1.2 The Authorised Person shall shut down the machine or equipment using normal stopping procedures (e.g., control console stop button, cycle finish state) to avoid creating additional hazards.

*   **Step 3: Isolation**
    3.1.3 The Authorised Person shall operate all energy-isolating devices (circuit breakers, disconnect switches, line valves, slide gates) to the "OFF" or "OPEN" position so that the equipment is disconnected from all primary energy sources.

*   **Step 4: Lockout and Tagout**
    3.1.4 The Authorised Person shall affix their Personal LOTO Padlock and completed "Danger – Do Not Operate" tag (Form VPW-TAG-01) to each energy-isolating device.
    3.1.5 Tags must be securely fastened with a single-use nylon cable tie and legibly filled out in permanent black ink with the employee name, payroll number, department, and date/time of application.

*   **Step 5: Stored Energy Dissipation and Verification**
    3.1.6 The Authorised Person shall relieve, disconnect, restrain, or otherwise render safe all residual or stored energy in accordance with Section 4 of this SOP.
    3.1.7 The Authorised Person shall verify that isolation and energy dissipation are complete by attempting to operate normal controls (e.g., pushbuttons, selector switches) and observing zero mechanical movement or electrical indication, followed by instrument verification.

*   **Step 6: Maintenance Execution**
    3.1.8 The equipment is verified as zero-energy and safe for maintenance work to commence.

### 4. STORED-ENERGY VERIFICATION AND DISSIPATION
4.1 **Electrical Systems:**
    4.1.1 Following breaker opening and padlock application, the Authorised Person shall verify zero voltage at all phase terminals using a category CAT IV 1000V digital multimeter calibrated within the preceding 180 days.
    4.1.2 Testing must follow the three-point protocol: test a known live source, test the target equipment terminals, and re-test the known live source to confirm meter functionality.
    4.1.3 Capacitors shall be discharged using an approved VPW grounding wand for a minimum of sixty (60) seconds after power disconnection.

4.2 **Pneumatic Systems:**
    4.2.1 Close the main pneumatic supply isolation ball valve and apply LOTO.
    4.2.2 Open the downstream manual bleed-off valve or three-way exhaust valve to depressurise the circuit.
    4.2.3 Verify zero pressure by reading the local line pressure gauge. The needle must rest solidly at 0.0 bar/psi for a minimum observation period of thirty (30) seconds.

4.3 **Hydraulic Systems:**
    4.3.1 Shut off the hydraulic power unit (HPU) pump motor and apply LOTO to the main electrical disconnect.
    4.3.2 Close the high-pressure accumulator isolation valves.
    4.3.3 Open the manual pressure-relief bleed valve routed to the return tank. Allow accumulator bladders/pistons to discharge fully.
    4.3.4 Verify zero hydraulic pressure via the analog gauge mounted downstream of the relief valve. Pressure must register 0.0 MPa/bar.
    4.3.5 Mechanically pin or lock out any suspended hydraulic cylinders using rated mechanical safety props (VPW-PROP-SERIES).

### 5. REMOVAL OF LOCKS AND TAGS
5.1 **General Rule:** Only the Authorised Person who applied a Personal LOTO Padlock and tag may remove it. No other person, supervisor, or plant manager has authority to remove another technician’s padlock, subject to the single named exception in Clause 5.2.
5.2 **Single Named Exception (Emergency Padlock Removal):**
    5.2.1 If the Authorised Person who applied the lock has left the plant premises, is incapacitated, or is otherwise unreachable, their padlock may be removed *only* under the personal authorisation of the Plant Operations Director, Mr. Rajesh Nair (or his formally designated acting deputy, Plant EHS Manager Ms. Sunita Patel).
    5.2.2 Before emergency removal is authorised, the following four conditions must be met cumulatively:
        a) Management has made documented, verifiable attempts to contact the absent padlock owner via telephone and messaging over a mandatory thirty (30) minute window.
        b) A thorough physical inspection of the work zone has been conducted by the Maintenance Engineering Lead, confirming that all tools are cleared, guards are restored, and no personnel are exposed.
        c) The absent employee is notified via SMS and email prior to plant entry on their next scheduled shift regarding the lock removal.
        d) Form VPW-EHS-042B (Emergency LOTO Removal Authorisation) is completed, signed by Mr. Rajesh Nair (or Ms. Sunita Patel), and lodged with the EHS Control Room within sixty (60) minutes of removal.

### 6. GROUP LOCKOUT PROCEDURE
6.1 When a maintenance or overhaul task requires three or more Authorised Persons from different trades (e.g., mechanical, electrical, and instrumentation) to work on the same interconnected asset, a Group Lockout procedure must be implemented.
6.2 The designated Lead Authorised Person (LAP) for the shift shall coordinate the group lockout.
6.3 The LAP shall perform the six-step isolation sequence (Steps 1 through 5) on all energy sources feeding the asset.
6.4 The LAP shall apply their master group lock(s) and a green group tag to each primary isolation point.
6.5 The keys to the master group locks shall be placed inside a red Group Lockout Box.
6.6 Every participating Authorised Person working on the asset shall affix their personal red LOTO padlock and tag to the hasp of the Group Lockout Box before commencing work.
6.7 As individual technicians complete their assigned scope of work, they shall remove their personal padlock from the Group Lockout Box.
6.8 The LAP shall not remove the master group locks from the primary energy isolation points until all personal padlocks have been removed from the Group Lockout Box, confirming every technician is clear of the hazard zone.
